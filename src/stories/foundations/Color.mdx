# Color

The interface is achromatic. Depth is a ladder of values: the **canvas** is the page, **surfaces** sit one step lighter, **recessed** fields one step darker. Color enters only through the orb and small status signals.

## Status

Small doses: a 6px dot, a short colored word, or a /10 tint. Never a filled button.
